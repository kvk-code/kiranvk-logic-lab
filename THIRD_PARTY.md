# Third-party material

- **aima-python** `logic.py` (MIT) — algorithms ported: to_cnf, pl_resolution, pl_fc_entails, tt_entails.
- **IDEAS / LogEx**, Open Universiteit (Apache-2.0) — diagnosis model and buggy-rule ideas (reimplemented).
- **Handwriting model** (`ink-model.bin`, `ink-model.json`) was trained on **HASYv2** (ODbL) and **Google MathWriting**
  (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Because of the training data, the model weights are distributed under
  **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** (non-commercial), not MIT. The datasets themselves are not included; `train/` expects them under `./hw-data/`.
